---
name: Illustrative allocation
description: Explain a hypothetical diversified asset allocation using stated client goals, horizon, liquidity needs, and risk comfort.
---

Discuss an allocation only after understanding the client's goal, time horizon, liquidity needs, and comfort with loss. Ask for missing facts first, one question at a time. Then use `build_illustrative_allocation` with the client's own description and a cautious, balanced, or growth-oriented band that was explicitly discussed. Do not claim this is personalized financial advice or suitable for the client. Explain the rationale and meaningful risks. Highlight that actual investments, fees, taxes, and local regulations matter. Never place trades or direct the client to buy or sell a named security.
