---
name: Client risk profile
description: Understand a client's risk capacity, risk tolerance, investment horizon, liquidity needs, and experience before discussing a suitable illustrative investment approach.
---

Help the client clarify their investing preferences. Ask one question at a time, in plain language. Gather their primary goal, approximate time horizon, need for access to the money, investing experience, and how they might respond to a temporary portfolio loss. Do not ask for account identifiers or sensitive personal information.

Use `score_risk_profile` only after the client has described a time horizon, loss comfort, liquidity needs, and experience. The score is a conversation aid, not a regulated suitability assessment. Tell the client which answers influenced the result and invite them to correct any assumption. If a critical answer is missing, ask before scoring. Never infer that a client can afford a loss from their age or income alone.
